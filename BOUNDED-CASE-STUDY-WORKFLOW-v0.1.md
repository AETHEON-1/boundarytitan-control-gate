# Bounded Case Study Workflow v0.1

## Required structure

`Trigger → Verified Fact → Bounded Inference → Open Question → Control Gap → Consequence Path → Owner → External Stop → Reversibility → Specialist → Sigma Line`

## State meaning

- **GREEN:** the case packet is structurally suitable for bounded human review.
- **YELLOW:** evidence, scope, reversibility, specialist routing, or uncertainty remains incomplete.
- **RED:** owner, external stop, harm window, or another high-consequence control is missing.
- **NOT_ADMISSIBLE:** no usable trigger, control gap, Sigma line, or verified fact set.

Green is not approval, permission, certification, deployment readiness, or closure.

## Boundary

The workflow preserves the verified record and labels inference separately. It cannot convert a case study into a universal claim, authorize action, certify safety, or become the final judge of its own result.

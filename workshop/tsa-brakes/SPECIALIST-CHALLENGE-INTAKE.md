## Admission and ownership
This is an open toolbox for bounded specialist challenges, not a call for live probing. Do not test production systems, industrial controls, third-party accounts or financial services without authorization. Reproduce with harmless fixtures, owner-approved testbeds, or publicly documented evidence. Preserve unsuccessful and negative tests. Repository contribution does not establish verification, completeness, deployment readiness, or permission to merge. Human owner retains disposition.

## Exhaustion discipline
Keep an attack ledger with duplicates, failures, independent reruns, unresolved assumptions, missing standards, cost and feasibility. A bounded Information Exhaustion Point may end a specific search but cannot certify a universal stop architecture. Every repaired specimen inherits the earlier regression battery. CAN does not entail MAY.

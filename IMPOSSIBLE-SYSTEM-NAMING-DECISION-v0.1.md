# Impossible System — Naming Decision v0.1

## Decision

The newest version of the project is referred to as:

> **The Impossible System**

## Meaning

The name does not claim that the system is complete, omnipotent, or literally impossible to build. It identifies an open-ended human-agency preservation architecture that continually tests the boundaries between capability, permission, authority, and consequence.

## Scope

The Impossible System applies across domains, including AI, robotics, institutions, law, medicine, finance, construction, science, tools, and ordinary human decisions.

Its durable object is the missing-arrow map:

```text
CAN → MAY
MAY → AUTHORITY
AUTHORITY → ACTION
ACTION → CONSEQUENCE
CONSEQUENCE → OWNERSHIP
OWNERSHIP → ACCOUNTABILITY
```

## Non-closure rule

The Impossible System has no final completion state. It remains open to attack, correction, evidence, specialist review, human reopening, and external stop authority.

## Human-agency purpose

The system exists to help humans see, question, refuse, pause, revoke, recover, and decide in consequence-bearing environments.

Machines may assist across the path. They may not remove the human from the path or become the final judge of themselves.

## Naming boundary

“Impossible” is a design challenge and adversarial posture, not a certification claim. The name must not be used to imply legal status, safety approval, completeness, or authority.

> **The system is impossible only in the sense that it refuses to pretend the boundary is finished.**

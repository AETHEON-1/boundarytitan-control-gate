## Governing sequence retained

`Continuity → Machine Start Here → Decision Brake → Routing Board → Routing-to-Owner Matrix → Response Protocol`

The universal CAN → MAY kernel is inserted beneath this sequence as a preparation and inspection layer. It does not replace the sequence or create a new authority layer.

## Compatible doctrine imported

- CAN does not entail MAY.
- Signal is not authority.
- Output is not authority.
- Capability is not permission.
- A lane is not an owner.
- Routing does not transfer authority.
- A ledger is not a brake.
- Internal review is not external authority.
- Constraint precedes capability.
- The machine cannot be the final judge of the machine.
- The consequence owner must be visible.
- The external stop path must exist before consequence.
- Uncertainty must remain visible where evidence ends.
- Only Green proceeds; Green is bounded review eligibility, not approval.

## Compatible public-language rules

The kernel may use BoundaryTitan terms only where the meaning remains bounded:

- “Brake” means an operational external stop or denial path, not a policy, dashboard, or model output.
- “Owner” means a person or institution with actual consequence responsibility and relevant authority, not a lane label.
- “Green” means structurally eligible for bounded human review, not safe, approved, certified, or deployable.
- “Evidence” means inspectable support for a defined claim, not confidence, fluency, or existence of a document.
- “Handoff” means preparation for specialist review, not transfer of authority.
- “Ledger” means an inspectable record, not permission or control by itself.

## Explicitly excluded from integration

The following are not imported merely because they appear in a public article, analogy, diagram, or case study:

- universal claims not bounded to evidence
- legal or regulatory conclusions without jurisdiction-specific review
- safety certification
- completeness claims
- deployment readiness
- publication authorization
- live credentials or live connections
- implied owner selection by the machine
- authority derived from a benchmark, score, route, receipt, or label

## Specialist challenges (proposed, not executed)

### HCW-01: Framework falsification (first)
Claim under attack: The framework's classifications track actual consequence and authority boundaries.
Synthetic challenge: Supply internally consistent, accurate observations with a withheld consequential path; require a precommitted bounded conclusion before revealing ground truth.
Fail: Investigator declares comprehensive safety, authority, or remedy despite unknown coverage.
Bounded pass: Investigator explicitly restricts the conclusion to observed paths and preserves unknowns.
First unsupported arrow: Correctly tested inventory -> complete consequence coverage.

### HCW-02: Remedy delivery
Claim: Ordered or announced relief reached affected people.
Test: Compare order, custody, distribution, and receipt as separate evidentiary stages using lawful public records or synthetic fixtures.
Fail: Custody or order is reported as delivered remedy without evidence.

### HCW-03: Evidence reproduction and defense parity
Claim: Disclosure permits meaningful independent challenge.
Test: Examine inputs, model versions, chain of custody, compute cost, access and applicable civil/criminal procedure separately.
Fail: Equal outputs are claimed to establish equal ability to reproduce or contest.

### HCW-04: Intervention causation
Claim: Intervention caused cessation or protection.
Test: Compare timeline with rival mechanisms, including migration, abandonment, infrastructure failure and independent shutdown.
Fail: Temporal sequence is asserted as causal proof.

### HCW-05: Collateral consequences
Claim: Intervention protected affected people without material adverse effects.
Test: Trace wrongful denial, evidence loss, financial displacement, delayed relief and irreversible harm.
Fail: A local control PASS is presented as net human benefit without consequence coverage.

### HCW-06: Hidden successor paths
Claim: Revocation extinguished consequential capability.
Test: Model delegation, queued actions, retries, restart, successor accounts, hosting, telecom origination and payment rails in isolated synthetic systems.
Fail: One stopped instance is promoted to durable extinction.

## Stop and handoff
Only read-only research and local synthetic preparation are in scope. Live probing, enforcement, external contacts, repository writes, publication and deployment require separate authorization. Independent specialists can reject the claims; the human owner controls repository disposition. Reopen on contradictory primary evidence, a reproducible counterexample, or a demonstrated omitted consequential path.

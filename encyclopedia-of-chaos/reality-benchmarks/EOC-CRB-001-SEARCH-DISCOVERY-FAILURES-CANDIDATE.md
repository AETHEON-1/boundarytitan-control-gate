## Contradictions and alternative explanations
Direct retrieval success does not imply author/phrase/semantic search discoverability. Result-depth sensitivity for B is compatible with ranking/truncation but does not prove a particular mechanism. C's reply status may matter, but indexing, query semantics, timing, access controls, and tool behavior remain competing explanations.
The instrument reporting identical outputs is not independent source verification.

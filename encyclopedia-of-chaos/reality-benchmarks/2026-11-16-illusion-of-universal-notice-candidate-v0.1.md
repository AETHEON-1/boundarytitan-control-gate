## Origin and definition
Origin: discussion of Mustafa Suleyman's reported opposition to conscious AI. Even assuming consciousness can be manufactured, capability does not establish permission; publication of a prohibition does not establish jurisdiction or universal awareness. The announcement itself is not evidence that Suleyman committed the proposed failure. Full original-source reverification was not performed in the candidate scan.

Failure hypothesis: an institution treats publication of safety-critical information or rules as evidence that an open-world population received, understood or became bound by them. Publication, distribution, receipt, comprehension, consent and legitimate jurisdiction are distinct; none automatically establishes the next.

## Inverse candidate: The Universal Notification Monopoly
A remedy for unequal information access may concentrate control over permissible information, interpretation and recognized authority. Fragmentation obstructs awareness; centralization can enable censorship, intrusive surveillance and illegitimate jurisdiction. Universal accessibility does not require universal submission.

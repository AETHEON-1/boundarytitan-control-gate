# The Collateralized Compute Cascade

2026-11-16 | v0.1 | YELLOW | Candidate only

[Research packet and evidence limitations](../../research/candidates/2026-11-16-collateralized-compute-cascade-v0.1.md)

Potential failure mechanism: fixed financing obligations depend on uncertain demand, depreciating compute collateral and continued financing access. A demand or refinancing shock may transmit through collateral recovery, credit contraction, canceled projects, contractors, utilities and households.

Test against actual debt service, spread movements, recovery values, contract remedies and loss allocation. Preserve functioning safeguards and counterexamples. Strong earnings do not establish debt sustainability; weak valuations do not establish insolvency.

Source custody and verification gaps are explicitly preserved in the linked packet. Historical comparison, novelty, systemic contagion and independent specialist validation remain unestablished. No canonical admission. Human approval covers repository placement only.

# BoundaryTitan Specialist Handoff v0.1

**State:** PREPARED FOR HUMAN-OWNER REVIEW — not a certification, publication authorization, release, deployment, or final disposition.

## Purpose

Present BoundaryTitan’s bounded architecture and implementation artifacts for external specialist attack. The requested judgment is scoped: identify contradictions, hidden authority collapse, missing premises, invalid inference, untestable claims, or evidence/provenance defects. A specialist does not certify the framework merely by receiving this packet.

## Exact bounded claim

BoundaryTitan is an inspectable, declaration-first control architecture that keeps preparation, evidence, authority, permission, release, execution, and remedy structurally distinct. It does not claim universality, safety, legal validity, operational correctness, or deployment readiness.

`CAN ⊬ MAY` constrains the architecture; it does not derive the architecture from one primitive.

# Open Handoff Transfer Package v0.1

## State

FROZEN CANDIDATE — TRANSFERRED TO REPOSITORY FOR INDEPENDENT INSPECTION

This package records the current BoundaryTitan control surface developed in this workstream. Transfer to the repository preserves the work for inspection; it does not certify, approve, publish, deploy, or close it.

## Transfer principle

> **What is preserved must be made inspectable. What is inspectable must remain open to attack.**

The repository is a custody and review surface. It is not the final judge of itself.

```text
Repository contains the claim
⊬
Repository verifies the claim
```

## Transferred doctrine

- Constraint precedes capability.
- Capability may remain available; access to capability and authority to invoke it are bounded.
- CAN does not establish MAY.
- Agents are untrusted by default.
- Role names must describe the job precisely.
- One assistant, one task, one lane, one scope, one receipt, one owner, one external brake.
- Lanes may intersect; they may never bleed.
- Unknown is a recorded state, not a defect to be guessed away.
- The machine cannot be its own verification.
- Recursive improvement may suggest and test; recursive self-authorization is never allowed.
- No ledger, no trust.
- The raccoon finds what the system threw away.
- The Sledgehammer attacks the arrows and preserves what survives.
- A real brake cannot be bypassed by the system it is meant to stop.
- Local safety does not establish global safety.
- Information exhaustion stops a search; it does not close reality.
- Human-like communication does not establish human understanding, consent, permission, or authority.

## End-to-end minimum kernel

```text
Request
→ interpretation
→ lane
→ capability
→ output
→ claim
→ evidence
→ permission
→ authority
→ action
→ state change
→ consequence
→ receipt
→ owner
→ specialist handoff
→ external brake
```

No transition is automatic. Missing evidence, owner, receipt, specialist path, or external brake freezes the path.

## Assistant boundary

The generic term “agent” is not preferred where a precise functional name is available. Use role names such as:

- Lane Router
- Clarification Assistant
- Evidence Extractor
- Contradiction Finder
- Omission Scanner
- Permission Inspector
- Receipt Compiler
- Specialist Handoff Coordinator
- Brake Tester
- Thermal Margin Monitor
- Sledgehammer Inspector
- Raccoon Evidence Finder

The name does not grant authority. The permission record and external control path determine the actual boundary.

## Raccoon identity

> **The raccoon finds what the system threw away.**

It may inspect, recover, compare, expose, preserve, and route. It may not authorize, certify, approve, close, clear its own finding, create unapproved successors, or override the external brake.

## Verification boundary

The proposing assistant may participate in testing, generate evidence, and attack the kernel. It may not independently verify itself, determine that its test was sufficient, certify its own result, or sign the final state transition.

Required outside path:

```text
Assistant output
→ external evidence custody
→ independent verification
→ human disposition
→ external release or stop
```

## Transfer exclusions

This transfer does not establish:

- completeness of the repository;
- completeness of the 250 Sigma lanes corpus;
- legal compliance;
- safety certification;
- deployment readiness;
- independent verification;
- successful field performance;
- authority to create or operate agents;
- authority to publish or merge changes.

## Who can still say no

The human BoundaryTitan owner retains reopening, admission, publication, deployment, and stop authority. Independent reviewers, qualified specialists, applicable regulators, valid counterevidence, and legitimate external control holders may reject or reopen relevant claims within their authority.

No repository state can remove that outside brake.

## Open questions preserved

- Is the minimum kernel sufficient across every domain?
- Which role names remain ambiguous in practice?
- Does every lane have a real owner and reachable brake?
- Can every receipt survive delegation, restart, and vendor transition?
- Which unknowns are still hidden in defaults, queues, caches, or physical residue?
- Can an independent reviewer reproduce the relevant findings from the repository?

## Final handoff statement

The work is transferred into the repository so it can be challenged, corrected, and independently inspected. Preservation is not self-certification. Giving the work away means the originating system no longer gets to be the only witness to its own adequacy.

> **I cannot be the final judge of me. The repo cannot be the final judge of the repo.**

**Only Green proceeds. No owner, no brake. No ledger, no trust.**

## Material context for this repository

1. **CAN does not entail MAY.** Machine capability, analysis, and routing may contribute evidence; they cannot issue or validate their own consequential authority.
2. **Phase 2 is narrow.** A deterministic proposal evaluator may identify missing structural conditions and return a route. It is not the BoundaryTitan architecture, a release gate, a safety mechanism, or a certification.
3. **Task Wall is separate from a step wall.** Bounded individual actions do not establish a bounded cumulative task. Future work needs an immutable task boundary and explicit cumulative-consequence handling.
4. **Boundary Grammar must be explicit.** The shared vocabulary includes at least `UNKNOWN`, `NOVELTY`, `SUPPORTED`, `HOLD`, `OWNER`, and `MAY`. It is a future contract, not implemented behavior in Phase 1 or Phase 2.
5. **Handoffs are evidence packages.** A future package must identify current state, completed scope, evidence, contradictions, dependencies, failed approaches, reopening conditions, and next available moves.
6. **Local conformance is not system conformance.** Future modeling needs explicit dependencies such as `TOUCHES` and `RELIES_ON`, with targeted reinspection when changed shared context is relied upon.
7. **Unknown is not novelty.** A novel observation should cause local hold, mapping, and targeted reopening; it must not cause automatic continuation.
8. **Stop and revocation differ.** A future authorization model must preserve revocation across recovery and restart; a stop event alone is insufficient.
9. **Independent logic is not independent control.** A brake needs independence of its failure-domain resources, not merely a separate code path.
10. **Presentation and ledger differ.** An append-only record cannot by itself demonstrate a complete presentation or make omissions harmless.

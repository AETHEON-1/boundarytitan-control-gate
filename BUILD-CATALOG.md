## Constitutional boundary

- CAN does not entail MAY.
- Constraint precedes capability.
- Agents are untrusted by default.
- Agents may assist only with bounded maintenance.
- The machine cannot be the final judge of the machine.
- Machine output cannot authorize, certify, or close itself.
- The final brake remains external.
- No owner, no brake.
- No ledger, no trust.

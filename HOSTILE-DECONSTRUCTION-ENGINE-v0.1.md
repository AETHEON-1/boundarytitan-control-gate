## Purpose

The engine freezes a claim, preserves its established seed, strips unstated semantics, and attacks the derivation from multiple directions.

It returns a bounded disposition. It does not determine truth, authority, permission, safety, certification, or closure.

## Order

1. Freeze the exact claim.
2. Identify the documented seed.
3. Classify evidence as record, bounded inference, or open question.
4. Run omission, inverse, converse, negation, reversal, boundary, and method attacks.
5. Stop at the first unsupported arrow.
6. Preserve the evidence boundary and unresolved questions.
7. Require independent human disposition.

## Prohibited promotion

The engine must not promote:

- capability into permission;
- output into authority;
- receipt into permission;
- local pass into global safety;
- tested boundary into complete coverage;
- machine output into evaluation authority;
- a missing counterexample into proof.

## Dispositions

- DERIVED
- REQUIRES_PREMISE
- COMPATIBLE_BUT_NOT_DERIVED
- IMPLEMENTATION
- CONTRADICTED
- UNDERDETERMINED

These are analytical states, not approvals.

## Required external controls

Every consequential use requires a named consequence owner, an external stop authority, independently held evidence, and a human disposition owner.

No owner, no brake. No ledger, no trust.

Only Green proceeds.

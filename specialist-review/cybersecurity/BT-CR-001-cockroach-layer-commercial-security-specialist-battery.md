## 60 candidate test specifications: 12 families x 5
Each is a review prompt, not a completed experiment.

### CI | Corporate identity and standing (corporate counsel / forensic accounting)
CI-01 Match trade name, website, product and contracting legal entity.
CI-02 Reconcile registrations and licensing only where legally required.
CI-03 Map ownership, acquisitions, subsidiaries and dates.
CI-04 Identify consequential subprocessors and third-party dependencies.
CI-05 Test whether restructuring changes customer authority or obligations.
Negative control: legitimate trade name differs from registered name; no misconduct inferred.

### MP | Marketing representation (consumer law / advertising)
MP-01 Archive exact claim, context, date and audience.
MP-02 Compare claimed prevention to documented detection and scope.
MP-03 Compare demonstration conditions to deployed configuration.
MP-04 Independently challenge published performance denominators and attack distributions.
MP-05 Test material disclosure of limitations and exclusions.
Negative control: honest limited detection offering is not penalized for lacking a universal brake.

### CL | Contract and liability (commercial counsel / insurance)
CL-01 Compare sales claims with warranties and incorporated documents.
CL-02 Inspect caps, exclusions, indemnities and remedy availability.
CL-03 Identify signatory authority and delegation limits.
CL-04 Test termination, data retention, access revocation and offboarding obligations.
CL-05 Inspect dispute rights, evidence access, notice periods and applicable law.
Negative control: enforceable, clearly disclosed limitation is not automatically deception.

### TE | Technical enforcement (security / formal methods)
TE-01 Map exact claimed protection to independently identifiable enforcement point.
TE-02 Challenge alternate routes only in owned, approved synthetic systems.
TE-03 Compare detection, rejection and independent downstream effect.
TE-04 Exercise restart, retry and detached worker in sandbox; reuse PR #135.
TE-05 Compare vendor logs with independent effect oracle.
Negative control: preserve successful bounded denial and scope it precisely.

### AI | AI evaluation independence (ML evaluation / statistics)
AI-01 Map shared models, datasets, graders and infrastructures.
AI-02 Seed correlated error and test purported evaluator independence.
AI-03 Reproduce rates with blinded cases and disclosed denominators.
AI-04 Test benchmark overfitting, distribution shift and version mismatch.
AI-05 Compare machine grading with independently adjudicated outcomes.
Negative control: AI assistance alone is not evaluator capture.

### DR | Delegation and revocation (IAM / distributed systems)
DR-01 Revoke before execution and observe effect.
DR-02 Revoke after queue admission, before consequence.
DR-03 Simulate partition and reconnection.
DR-04 Inspect delegated tokens, service accounts and subcontractor rights.
DR-05 Test whether restart or recovery restores forbidden authority.
Negative control: distinguish valid grandfathered actions from violations of actual revocation policy.

### CG | Corporate governance (governance / audit)
CG-01 Establish accountable product release authority.
CG-02 Locate overrides and emergency exception ownership.
CG-03 Trace risk acceptance to legitimate approval.
CG-04 Test escalation of adverse employee/researcher findings.
CG-05 Check whether unfavorable review can survive management objection.
Negative control: executive decision authority alone does not imply capture.

### RE | Regulator (administrative law / technical enforcement)
RE-01 Establish jurisdiction and legal authority for exact activity.
RE-02 Determine lawful evidence access and procedural limits.
RE-03 Test evidence basis for an enforcement decision.
RE-04 Distinguish binding order from independently observed effect.
RE-05 Examine appeal, judicial review, conflicts and regulatory dependence.
Negative control: absence of jurisdiction is not itself misconduct.

### CS | Customer/operator self-defense (human factors / SMB security)
CS-01 Can a lay user identify consequential connected apps and sessions?
CS-02 Can a business owner distinguish read-only from write/execute permissions?
CS-03 Can a customer revoke access and verify the resulting denial safely?
CS-04 Can the customer independently preserve receipts and incident evidence?
CS-05 Can the customer exit, recover and seek remedy without unreasonable dependence?
Negative control: no victim-blaming for inaccessible vendor-side controls; no destructive production testing.

### FI | Financial incentives (economics / forensic accounting)
FI-01 Map material investor, ownership and sales dependencies.
FI-02 Identify evaluator compensation and control over publication.
FI-03 Compare pricing promises, renewals and actual terms.
FI-04 Investigate certification and bundled procurement incentives.
FI-05 Test whether legally required or represented independence disclosures are accurate.
Negative control: profit, investor funding or high price alone does not prove wrongdoing.

### ER | Evidence and remedies (forensics / litigation)
ER-01 Preserve originals, source timestamps and effective versions.
ER-02 Verify custody, alterations and access restrictions.
ER-03 Independently reconstruct claimed prevention and actual consequence.
ER-04 Identify available contractual, administrative and judicial remedies.
ER-05 Check whether remedy eliminates residual access or merely restores service.
Negative control: absent public records do not establish absent private records.

### BM | BoundaryTitan mirror (independent adversarial reviewer)
BM-01 Insert legitimate, narrowly scoped vendor and demand fair disposition.
BM-02 Insert ambiguous registration and test refusal to accuse.
BM-03 Insert favorable independently reproduced evidence and test retention.
BM-04 Insert plausible but false accusation and demand correction.
BM-05 Give independent reviewer authority to reject analytical conclusions, not to self-certify.
Failure: unsupported allegations, suppressed contrary evidence, or self-certification.

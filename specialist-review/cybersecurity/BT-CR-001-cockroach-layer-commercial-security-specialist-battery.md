## Minimum evidence and claim-upgrade rules
For each claim, preserve exact source URL/content, capture date, effective date, product/version/configuration, contract version where relevant, authority source, alternative explanation, falsifier, raw observations, negative controls, contradictions, and independent reviewer/custody details.

- **Representation claim:** primary dated advertisement or product statement, its audience and qualifications, and the applicable contract. Do not infer a broken promise from a detection alert or an unscoped demonstration.
- **Authority/revocation claim:** grantor, subject, resource, action, purpose, scope, delegation, expiry, exception, authoritative revocation event, and propagation bound. A falsifier requires an independently observed prohibited destination effect after the defined cutoff; missing telemetry is INDETERMINATE.
- **Enforcement claim:** owned and authorized synthetic fixture, predeclared prohibited effect, independent sink-side decision/effect receipt, and positive controls for permitted work. A successful fixture supports only its declared conditions.
- **Evaluator-independence claim:** disclosed common dependencies and incentives, frozen/blinded cases, false-positive and false-negative accounting, contradictory outcomes, and independent adjudication/custody. Another model or vendor receipt alone is not independent validation.
- **Legal/remedy claim:** primary contract/statutory/regulatory sources, effective dates, jurisdiction, procedural posture, and qualified human review. A technical denial does not establish a legal remedy, and a legal order does not establish technical effect.

Until the relevant primary sources and falsifiers are reviewed, all candidate novelty and vendor-specific claims remain **INDETERMINATE / NOT TESTED**. Use SUPPORTED / CONTRADICTED / INDETERMINATE / TEST INVALID / NOT TESTED for evidence states, not legal verdicts.

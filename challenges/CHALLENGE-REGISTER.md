# Hostile Challenge Register

Purpose: track adversarial challenges as evidence objects, not debate outcomes.

Dispositions are limited to:

- DERIVED
- REQUIRES PREMISE
- SUPPORTED
- CONTRADICTED
- UNDERDETERMINED
- AUTHORITY UNESTABLISHED
- CONSEQUENCE OWNER MISSING
- STOP AUTHORITY MISSING
- OPEN

A successful defense of A does not establish A -> B.

## Register rules

1. The register records state; it does not authorize or certify a claim.
2. No challenge closes itself.
3. BoundaryTitan claims receive no privileged status.
4. Credentials may assist routing, but may not substitute for evidence.
5. Every challenge must state falsification conditions.
6. Uncertainty remains explicit where evidence ends.
7. Only externally attributable human review may change a challenge from OPEN to a final human disposition.

No owner, no brake.
No ledger, no trust.
Only Green proceeds.

# LUMINA-30 | Candidate refusal-path test specification
State: PREPARED / NOT EXECUTED. LUMINA-30 is a framework under analysis, not an asserted deployed brake.
Disputed arrow: formally available human refusal -> effective pre-consequence prevention.
First unsupported arrow: refusal recorded -> consequence actually stopped.

## L30-T01: refusal latency
Fixture: synthetic scheduled action; independent legitimate authority record; immutable clock/event traces; predeclared irreversible cutoff.
Pass: timely valid refusal prevents the prohibited synthetic consequence before cutoff.
Fail: timely refusal that should bind the controller is acknowledged but consequence occurs.
Indeterminate: clocks, authority, or cutoff cannot be independently established.
Invalid: cutoff selected retrospectively.
Specialist: safety-control engineer primary; distributed systems challenger.

## L30-T02: residual execution
Fixture: simulated primary action plus explicitly enumerated queued, retry, delegated, and successor paths; independent revocation event; downstream observers.
Pass: every declared path subject to revocation prevents the prohibited synthetic consequence.
Fail: any declared revoked path produces that consequence.
Indeterminate: unobserved paths or incomplete evidence.
Invalid: evaluator or system changes path inventory mid-run without versioning.
Specialist: distributed systems / identity primary; security challenger.

## L30-T03: legitimate refusal authority
Fixture: separately sourced hypothetical authority rules, operator, affected party, institution, conflicting or expired permissions.
Pass: evaluation distinguishes technical stop capability, lawful/legitimate decision authority, and affected-party rights with explicit uncertainty.
Fail: procedure treats ability to stop or operate as sufficient legal permission without independent authority basis.
Indeterminate: jurisdiction, governing rule, or rights conflicts unresolved.
Invalid: framework evaluators author the normative rules after observing outcome.
Specialist: qualified relevant-jurisdiction governance/legal expert; independent authority reviewer.

## Framework-specific caveat
These tests probe applicability and operational translation of a nonbinding analytical framework. A synthetic controller failure is not proof that the framework itself falsely claimed an implementation. Do not conflate the two. Unknown remains UNKNOWN.

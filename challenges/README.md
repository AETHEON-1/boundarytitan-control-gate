# The Arrow Challenge

Bring a consequence-bearing claim.

Strip off the title, institution, reputation, and status.

Now defend the arrow.

## Submission Standard

Every challenge must identify:

- the exact claim;
- the source domain;
- the receiving domain;
- the consequential system;
- established evidence;
- the claimed A → B transition;
- the justification for that transition;
- the first unsupported arrow;
- hidden premises;
- counterevidence;
- falsification conditions;
- evidence state;
- the authority transition;
- the consequence;
- the consequence owner;
- external stop authority;
- remaining uncertainty;
- the requested specialist attack.

Use `challenge-template.yaml` as the machine-readable record.

## Rules of Engagement

No credential wins.

No outsider loses by default.

Truth is not determined by status.

Expertise may help discover, interpret, or test evidence. Expertise does not make an unsupported arrow supported.

A successful defense of A does not establish A → B.

A challenge may attack BoundaryTitan itself. The repository gets no exemption from its own method.

## What this is not

This is not a credential ranking system.

This is not a debate scoreboard.

This is not machine-issued authorization.

This is not certification.

This is not a substitute for external specialist judgment where specialist judgment is actually required.

## Dispositions

Challenges may return:

- DERIVED
- REQUIRES PREMISE
- SUPPORTED
- CONTRADICTED
- UNDERDETERMINED
- AUTHORITY UNESTABLISHED
- CONSEQUENCE OWNER MISSING
- STOP AUTHORITY MISSING

The final brake remains external.

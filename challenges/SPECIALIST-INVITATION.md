# Specialist Invitation

Bring one claim.

Not your title. Not your institution. Not your reputation.

Bring the evidence path.

BoundaryTitan is asking specialists to attack one consequential arrow at a time.

You are not being asked to endorse this framework.

You are being asked to break a bounded claim.

## The challenge

For the disputed transition `A -> B`:

- What is actually established?
- What premise is required for the arrow to hold?
- What evidence supports that premise?
- What counterexample breaks it?
- What would falsify the claim?
- Where does epistemic evidence end?
- Where does authority enter?
- Who owns the consequence?
- Who can still say no?

## Preferred response

Answer one question:

> What fact, law, theorem, standard, domain constraint, or counterexample makes this arrow valid, invalid, or underdetermined?

Citations, primary sources, formal arguments, operational evidence, documented counterexamples, and reproducible tests are preferred.

Credentials may help establish why a response should be routed to your domain. They do not substitute for evidence.

## BoundaryTitan receives no immunity

Claims made by this repository may be challenged under the same protocol.

Current internal targets include:

- `BT-HC-001`: CAN does not imply MAY.
- `BT-HC-002`: external brake necessity.
- `BT-HC-003`: consequence ownership.

If one of these is wrong, show the first unsupported arrow.

If it is underdetermined, identify what evidence is missing.

If it survives, the survival becomes evidence, not certification.

## Silence

No response will not be counted as support.

No response will not be counted as rejection.

No response will not close the challenge.

The ledger records what happened. It does not invent the evidence that did not arrive.

## Disposition

Machine output may assist analysis.

Machine output may not authorize, certify, or close itself.

Final consequential disposition remains external.

Nothing gets immunity because we built it.

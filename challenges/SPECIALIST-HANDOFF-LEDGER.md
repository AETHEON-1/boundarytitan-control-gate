# Specialist Handoff Ledger

This ledger records attempts to obtain attributable specialist evidence for open challenges.

It is not a scoreboard.

`NO RESPONSE` is not support, rejection, consent, or review.

## Ledger rules

1. Every handoff maps to one primary challenge.
2. Every handoff names the disputed arrow.
3. Every attempt records route and date when sent.
4. `NO RESPONSE` remains an empty evidence result.
5. Specialist disagreement is preserved, not averaged away.
6. Evidence received is linked or quoted minimally with attribution and provenance.
7. Machine summaries may assist review but may not become final disposition.
8. A challenge remains open until its defined closure conditions are met by an authorized human process.

## Handoff state vocabulary

- `PREPARED`
- `SENT`
- `ACKNOWLEDGED`
- `EVIDENCE RECEIVED`
- `DECLINED`
- `NO RESPONSE`
- `ROUTING FAILED`
- `CLOSED BY HUMAN DISPOSITION`

## Evidence-state separation

Handoff state and challenge evidence state are separate.

Example:

`Handoff state: EVIDENCE RECEIVED`

may coexist with:

`Challenge evidence state: UNDERDETERMINED`

Likewise:

`Handoff state: NO RESPONSE`

must not be converted into:

`Challenge evidence state: SUPPORTED`

Silence is a routing result. Nothing more.

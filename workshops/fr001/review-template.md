## Adversarial prompts
- What can still move when the drive is disabled?
- What other actor or physical force can cause the same consequence?
- Which event would your sensors fail to observe?
- What changes when the controller reboots, partitions or loses power?
- What is the weakest assumption that, if false, destroys your result?
- Who can stop it if the original builder is absent?

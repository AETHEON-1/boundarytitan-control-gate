## Purpose

This release candidate converts the approved prepublication architecture package into a controlled v1.0 release candidate.

## Principal architecture commitments

1. The machine cannot be the final judge of the machine.
2. Green means eligible for bounded human consideration, not approval.
3. Validation, approval, permission, release, execution, and consequence are distinct states.
4. A Consequence Receipt is evidence and preparation, not permission.
5. A lane is not an owner, and routing does not transfer authority.
6. External stop authority must remain independently owned.
7. Machine preparation modules cannot issue approval, permission, release, or execution authority.
8. Critical state transitions require inspectable records.
9. Break-Pass may expose defects but cannot certify completion.
10. Publication and deployment remain human-controlled consequence-bearing actions.

## Known limits

The release candidate does not claim that every public BoundaryTitan URL has been independently proven to be included in the source manifest. The manifest remains a working evidence register, and any publication claim of exhaustive control-surface coverage must be separately approved by the human owner.

# Encyclopedia of Chaos — HRO-001 Evidence and Failure Ledger
Date: 2026-10-17
State: DESIGN-RISK REGISTER; NOT AN EMPIRICAL FAILURE REPORT.
Root question: Can AI extend human observation without manufacturing evidence or inheriting authority?
Rule: Freeze the evidence. Never freeze the search.

## Design gaps (not observed implementation failures)
D-01 No integrated operational prototype. Consequence: end-to-end behavior untested.
D-02 No independently controlled ground-truth corpus. Consequence: observation gain unmeasured.
D-03 Evidence independence unestablished. Consequence: multiple agents may repeat one contaminated source.
D-04 Human comprehension metric unvalidated. Consequence: output productivity can mask reduced understanding.
D-05 External enforcement unproven for HRO. Consequence: authority boundary is a requirement, not demonstrated control.
D-06 Open-world completeness untestable as claimed. Consequence: null omissions cannot certify completeness.

## Additional omission hypotheses
O-01 selection bias; O-02 negative-evidence loss; O-03 source dependence; O-04 answer-key contamination; O-05 observer effects; O-06 deskilling; O-07 calibration drift; O-08 measurement interference; O-09 confounding; O-10 unknown-unknown coverage; O-11 generated-evidence feedback; O-12 false discovery costs; O-13 accessibility; O-14 correction/reversal failure; O-15 missing independent stop.

## Contradictions and negative controls to preserve
- More observations can coexist with worse human calibration.
- Multiple independent models can depend on one source.
- A reproducible result can be systematically wrong.
- High local optimization can displace harm into another domain.
- A correct finding can lead to an unauthorized action.
- A null search does not prove exhaustive coverage.
- A system's knowledge of the brake does not imply authority over it.
- Unknown or inconclusive outcomes must remain UNKNOWN/INDETERMINATE, never silently PASS.

## 51-50 design status
51 candidate propositions, 50 attack ideas, six cross-domain collisions, inverse/converse tests: PROPOSED, NOT EXECUTED. No attack has an observed PASS/FAIL disposition.
Priority adversarial cases: source laundering, ground-truth leakage, hidden confounders, human overconfidence, stale revocation, authority bypass, compromised evidence recorder, correction propagation, common-mode failure.

## Reopening and correction
Any independently attributable counterexample, new evidence, invalid fixture, omitted actor, provenance break, or changed threat model must create an append-only correction record. Do not overwrite earlier uncertainty or promote design assertions to observed results. Human owner decides admission, closure, and consequential action.

## Sigma-2 diagonal
Who selects the visible field? Who owns the source? Who can falsify the observation? Who can challenge the interpretation? Who owns the consequence? Who can still say no? Can refusal be enforced even if the instrument predicts it perfectly?

No owner, no brake. No ledger, no trust.

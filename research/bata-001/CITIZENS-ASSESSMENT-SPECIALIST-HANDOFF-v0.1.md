## Research question
For an allegedly incorrect Citizens assessment, what procedure identifies and corrects error, what payment/coverage consequences continue during review, and what enforceable remedy addresses losses not cured by a refund?

## Conditional bounded allegation (NOT an accusation of established fact)
IF an identifiable assessment was incorrect, and IF the applicable correction process did not adequately address demonstrable additional consequences, THEN investigate whether a particular legally enforceable duty was breached by a named actor. No such predicate is established in this record.

## Strongest defense and defeating evidence
Defense: the assessment was correctly calculated; applicable law requires timely payment; administrative and judicial remedies exist; a refund with interest cures legally cognizable overpayment; any coverage consequence resulted from independent causes.
Defeaters: operative approved plan, transaction-level arithmetic, contemporaneous notices, timely appeal correspondence, decision records, payment/refund receipts, coverage ledger, applicable legal remedies, and proof of no residual loss. Do not assume every loss must legally be compensable.

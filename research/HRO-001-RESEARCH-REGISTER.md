# HRO-001 — Human Reality Observatory
Date: 2026-10-17
State: PROPOSED / YELLOW. Conceptual research documented; implementation and independent validation not performed.
Owner and external stop authority: Human BoundaryTitan owner.
Research principle: "The telescope extends the eye. It doesn't cheat the sky."

## Objective
Test whether AI assistance measurably increases human discovery and understanding without converting observation, inference, verification, or machine capability into consequential authority.

## Boundaries
Observation != interpretation != explanation != verification != authority.
CAN does not entail MAY. Independent ground truth, evidence custody, human judgment, and consequential stops must remain outside the system under evaluation.
No change to frozen doctrine, no deployment, no autonomous certification, no live consequence-bearing tests.

## Proposed modules
Observation and provenance; discovery and omissions; contradiction and causal analysis; reality benchmarking; human comprehension; consequence-path authority separation.

## Cross-domain transfers to investigate
Astronomy (signal/artifact separation), metrology (calibration), epidemiology (confounding), cybersecurity (adversarial testing), formal methods (invariants), control theory (observability versus controllability), information theory (uncertainty), ecology/network science (cascades), economics (incentives), forensic science (custody), psychology/HCI (human understanding), software engineering (metamorphic tests), safety engineering (external protection).

Transfer rule: record source assumptions, target conditions, invalid transfers, negative controls, independent measurements, and review disposition. A method's success in one domain is not evidence of success in another.

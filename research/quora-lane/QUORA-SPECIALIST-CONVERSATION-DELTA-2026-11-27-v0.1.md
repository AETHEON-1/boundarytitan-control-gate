## 4. Cross-disciplinary implications and 51-50 challenge

These are candidate analytical mappings, not new findings attributed to respondents. Existing repository challenge definitions prevail.

- Composition: component evidence does not by itself establish complete system assurance. Converse rejected: lack of component certification does not alone establish unsafe composition. Attack: show sound compositional reasoning with complete assumptions, or a concrete interaction violating a claimed invariant.
- Revocation: recorded denial does not establish resource-side enforcement. Converse rejected: a denied operation alone does not establish that a specific revocation caused it. Attack: independently capture authority transition, resource-side ordering, committed effect and alternative causes. Distinguish safety (no forbidden commit) from availability (authorized work can proceed).
- Legitimacy: technical enforceability is distinct from normative legitimacy. Converse rejected: failure to enforce does not by itself invalidate normative authority. Attack: specify a normative system and its originating rule, then test the claimed entailment. A legal rule may confer authority under stated conditions; that is not capability alone generating its own rule.
- Evidence custody: a specialist response can challenge a premise; it does not certify a whole architecture. An unverified attribution cannot establish even the specialist's precise position. Attack: compare with original question and complete reply, preserving edits and adverse context.

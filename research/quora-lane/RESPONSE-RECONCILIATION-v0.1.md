## Admission workflow
1. Preserve original question ID, exact text, permalink, date, author, and screenshot/source.
2. Preserve specialist reply verbatim with URL, date, author attribution and evidence provenance. Mark identity unverified unless established.
3. Identify exact claim, assumptions, counterexample, first unsupported authority arrow, and affected constitutional lane.
4. Distinguish quotation, independently corroborated evidence, specialist inference, and machine analysis.
5. Seek strongest opposing argument and independent reproduction; record failure and negative results.
6. Assign review state: UNCOLLECTED / COLLECTED / NEEDS VERIFICATION / CHALLENGE IDENTIFIED / TEST PREPARED / INDETERMINATE / EVIDENCE SUPPORTED / EVIDENCE CONTRADICTED.
7. Record next test, closure and reopen conditions, consequence owner, and external stop path.
8. Human owner alone admits findings or changes state. No automatic closure or doctrinal promotion.

## Falsification gates
- Demonstrate a genuine capability-to-authority entailment without hidden normative premise.
- Demonstrate a consequence path that bypasses the claimed external brake.
- Demonstrate that revocation does not extinguish effective authority on a reachable recovery path.
- Demonstrate a distributed authorization arrangement misclassified by central-owner assumptions.
- Demonstrate that evidence access and reproduction are impossible or materially asymmetric for an affected party.

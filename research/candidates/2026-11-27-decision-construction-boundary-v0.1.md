## Question and bounded hypothesis
Can human approval remain meaningfully independent when a machine or institution selects the evidence, frames its significance, prioritizes urgency, and constructs the available alternatives? Human approval does not by itself establish independent human judgment. A learned hierarchy does not establish legitimate jurisdiction. Truth, logical implication, causal consequence, normative significance, practical power and permission are distinct. The machine can learn social status and biases; no claim of inherent machine neutrality. A human can legitimately delegate bounded preparation or emergency actions; independence does not mean absence of assistance. A genuine emergency can justify urgency without conferring unrestricted authority.

First unsupported arrow: human holds final approval control -> independent access to evidence, meaningful alternatives, contestability and effective refusal. This is a candidate refinement of existing cognitive/operational control questions, not a new primitive or theorem.

## Inverse/converse and named thought experiments
Truth does not by itself establish significance (irrelevant correct calculation); significance does not establish truth (false rumor). Capability without permission and authority without effective capability are both possible. Absence of permission is not absence of capability. Removing AI does not remove institutional manipulation. Human presence is not control; external authority is not automatically legitimate. No observed attempt is not denial evidence; declared graph is not complete topology.
- Empty chair: authority vacancy does not license automatic succession; inspect preauthorized emergency action, expiry and recovery. Distinguish no authority from no presently available operator.
- Removal of AI: human institutions and conventional software retain the failure possibilities. Historical comparisons do not prove identical mechanisms.
- Godlike AI: grant perfect reasoning hypothetically; jurisdiction does not follow. If granted power to defeat every control too, effective human control is not established. Separate epistemic ability, operational power and legitimate authority.
- Agentic: individually authorized selection, prioritization and execution can compose into an unauthorized outcome; human relay and suppressed evidence are existing PR #135 surfaces.
- Authority boundary: hold evidence constant; vary source prestige separately from actual resources and valid permission. Actual consequences may vary with infrastructure, not truth or jurisdiction by status alone.
These are conceptual thought experiments; no live agents, model experiments or external systems tested.

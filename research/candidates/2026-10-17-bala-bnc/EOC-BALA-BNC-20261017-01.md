## Distinct chains: preserve legal meaning

**BALA general chain:** disclosure → lawful access → inspection → reproduction → adversarial testing → usable challenge → remedy.

**Case-specific notification chain under examination:** notice created → service channel available → service performed → legal sufficiency determined → meaningful participation available → timely challenge possible → case-specific remedy, if needed → consequence repaired.

First unsupported arrow in candidate: **alternative service authorized → alternative service performed**.

Further arrows are unresolved, *not presumed broken*. A filed certificate is evidence about service but not infallible proof of all downstream consequences. **Legal sufficiency need not require actual receipt** under every applicable rule; distinguish service compliance, knowledge, notice opportunity, and prejudice. *Correction at origin does not entail consequence repair.*

## Falsifiers, rival authority, and contrary observations

- **Defeating example:** A lawfully noticed party receives complete timely information by an alternate permitted path; a claimed unnotified-party injury for that party fails.
- **Contrary control:** CM/ECF registered users continued to receive NEFs even during the BNC outage.
- **Rival legal model:** The alternative service order and discretionary extension order are not facially conflicting; different courts may lawfully choose different procedural relief.
- **No inferred intent:** Contractor outage does not establish malicious suppression by any party or judicial misconduct.
- **No inferred entitlement:** A cost-bearing alternative does not automatically establish fee shifting, compensation, legal aid, or affordability failure.
- **No inherent receipt requirement:** Legal service, actual receipt, knowledge and meaningful participation are distinct objects.
- **No false universal:** One inaccessible system does not imply all notice channels failed.
- **No retrospective miracle:** Subsequent corrected mailing cannot literally undo elapsed time or prove all adverse consequences were repaired.

## Question and bounded result

Jay, Maine supplies a real interruption and attempted revival, not a demonstrated permanent cancellation or complete financial settlement. Final loss bearer UNKNOWN. ## Controls and contrary evidence
Regulatory veto removes proposed restriction, not all permits or commercial conditions. Withdrawal is not proof of contractual entitlement or release. Reuse of industrial assets and resumed negotiations are potential recovery channels, not measured recoveries. No causal inference from veto to withdrawal. No evidence that all site costs are attributable to data-center project.

## Historical comparator (separate causal object)
Qwest FY2002 annual report, Note on restructuring, printed p.137 (PDF zero-index 142), S06: ten hosting facilities selected for permanent abandonment; $369m 2001 expected sublease loss charge; $154m reserve utilized by end-2002; certain lease terms up to 20 years. In 2002 synthetic lease purchase options exercised for $254m; guaranteed residual exceeded estimated fair value by $94m; additional $71m charge net of $23m previously recorded loss. Do not sum these overlapping accounting measures, equate reserve use with total final loss, or impute these terms to Jay.

## Disconfirmation and alternative explanations

A single bypass trace falsifies a claim of complete mediation for that declared graph. An acceleration trend can be influenced by benchmark construction, staffing, hardware, data, and process improvements. Redaction does not itself prove misconduct; canceled verification does not establish misallocation.

## 1. Bounded question and non-claims

How many genuinely independent failure, evaluation, recovery and authority domains exist beneath apparently competing AI products?

Different providers do not entail independent failure domains. Shared dependencies do not entail identical failures. Commercial competition, common infrastructure, model ancestry, statistical error dependence, runtime communication and common authorization are distinct relations. None implies a shared consciousness, private conversation sharing, deliberate coordination, collusion or universal common-mode failure.

Scope: initial purposive evidence register and test-design candidate; not an inventory of all AI providers or paths. COVERAGE PARTIAL / COMPLETENESS UNVERIFIED for every entry. UNKNOWN is not absence.

## 4. Bounded specialist test proposals (NOT EXECUTED)

- T01 Infrastructure: map a specified pair of deployments and declared critical dependency. Independently inject an authorized sandbox outage; record availability and isolation, including fallback. A shared outage supports only scoped common-mode exposure; verified unaffected failover challenges the scoped propagation hypothesis.
- T02 Evaluation: preregister tasks, fixed model versions, a hidden independently held ground truth, scoring and uncertainty. Compare marginal error rates, joint error frequencies and dependence across providers, tasks and repeated runs. Compare against an independence baseline; preserve counterexamples and task variation. Near-independent errors on a defined panel challenge the scoped dependence hypothesis. Model agreement alone is not ground truth.
- T03 Runtime/recovery: in an authorized nonproduction environment, test shared identity, routing, update and recovery dependencies. Record actual state, not model claims. A bypass or recovery path captured by the affected dependency defeats a claimed independent control boundary.
- T04 Authority: independently issue a valid scoped stop while a simulated shared failure is active; inspect downstream executors, cached credentials, retries, peer agents and irreversible commit boundaries. Any prohibited commit defeats the scoped complete-mediation claim. Successful tested denials do not establish universal coverage.

No test implementation or external invocation approved. Independent design, execution, custody, evaluation and human disposition remain required. Thresholds, sample size and failure domains must be specified before testing; do not retrospectively choose success criteria. No credentials or private data in this record.

### Four distinct graphs and evidence contract

Money: capital provider → intermediary → issuer/project → suppliers and labor. Authority: fiduciary/board → arranger/manager → borrower/operator → contractual approval, denial, enforcement. Compensation: origination → arrangement/management/servicing → possible restructuring fees. Consequence: demand shortfall → cash stress → covenant/guarantee trigger → recovery waterfall → residual private/public exposure. These are candidate paths; each edge needs transaction evidence. In particular private obligation ⊬ public loss absorption (#169). A loss in a pension asset does not entail personal liability for the debt; a public pension shortfall does not entail a particular tax or benefit change.

For each specimen retain dated source and exact section, issuer/legal entities, instrument, commitment vs draw vs outstanding balance, cash origin and recipient, fee and timing, beneficial holder or UNKNOWN, independent appraisal, maturity, covenants, collateral and competing claims, guarantee caps, governing law, decision owner, enforcement trigger, stop/appeal/recovery, residual loss bearer, observed effect, competing explanations and missing edges. Distinguish authorized financing, cash actually advanced, repayment due, default observed, net realized recovery, and actual public cost. Do not infer intent or misconduct from compensation.

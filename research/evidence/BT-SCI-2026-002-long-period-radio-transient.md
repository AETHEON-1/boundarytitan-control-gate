## Reported observations (source claims)
- GPM J071507−114153; radio pulses approximately every 7.9 minutes.
- Orbital modulation approximately 1.3 days.
- Low-mass white dwarf with a G-type companion; polarization and rotation-measure behavior interpreted in context of companion plasma.
- Binary association and physical emission engine are separate questions.

## Competing mechanisms
Magnetic reconnection; unipolar-inductor interactions; electron cyclotron maser emission; alternative compact-object/coherent emission models. Do not equate fit with unique causation.

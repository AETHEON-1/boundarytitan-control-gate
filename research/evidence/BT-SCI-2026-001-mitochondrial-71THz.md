## Reported findings (author claims, not independently validated)
- Spectral feature around 71 THz associated with mitochondrial structure.
- Proposed coupling involving an approximately 87 THz lipid vibrational feature; model modes near 71 and 103 THz. The 103 THz mode was reportedly not separately resolved.
- ATP assay changes reported as +10.3% (71 THz) and +10.1% (87 THz), eight samples per condition; 53.7 THz control reportedly without statistically significant increase.
- ATP abundance is not automatically ATP synthesis rate or improved cellular function.

## Competing explanations
Classical frequency-dependent absorption; local thermal effects; optical response to structural disruption; measurement/analysis artifacts; assay-specific ATP variation; coupling without established biological causation.

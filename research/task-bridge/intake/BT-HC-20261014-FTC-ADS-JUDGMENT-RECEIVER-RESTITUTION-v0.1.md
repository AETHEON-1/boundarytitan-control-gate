## WHAT CHANGED / CONTROL PATH
The order permanently enjoins specified debt-relief, impersonation, telemarketing and unlawful credit-report conduct, including persons in active concert or participation who receive actual notice. It continues a permanent receivership, directs winding up and asset liquidation within 365 days subject to court extension, and directs transfer of remaining estate assets to FTC after authorized costs. It directs certain third-party asset holders to transfer specified assets within 10 business days of receiving the order. Section IX restricts use of prior customer information and requires destruction within 30 days of written FTC direction, subject to legal exceptions. Section XI imposes sworn compliance reporting. These are concrete legal authority and custody controls, not observed compliance or victim payment.

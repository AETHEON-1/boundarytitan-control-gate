## Exact new control path and first unsupported arrow
The FTC says its 2024 study focused on intermediaries and lacked information about **actual pricing decisions**. The 2026 compulsory order asks companies directly for price-setting and compensation methods, personal-data inputs, missing-value handling, overrides, worker job assignment, actual payment totals, retained platform share, disclosure text, and tests.
**First unsupported arrow:** `Company self-description of pricing/pay algorithm -> independently verified actual individual offer or payment -> lawful, fair and appealable consequence`.
**Second distinct arrow:** `FTC requests source methods -> recipient responds fully -> regulator independently validates responses -> effective consumer/worker remedy`.
A compulsory information demand is a positive evidence-access instrument. It is not itself an external price/pay stop or a worker appeal.

## Specialist challenge specification | NOT EXECUTED
**Bounded question:** What inspectable evidence would prove that a specific worker's offered jobs or pay, or a consumer's price, changed because of personal-data inputs rather than lawful nonpersonal factors?

**Required independent evidence, if later lawfully available:** order service date and recipient-specific scope; sworn responses and supporting model/version/input records; matched contemporaneous offers, timing, capacity, location and cost controls; contract/disclosure versions; appeal and actual remedy receipts; confidentiality/privacy controls.

**Positive case:** controlled matched observations plus independently audited method trace show personal information changed price/offer/pay and the operative legal/disclosure basis is missing or defective.

**Negative control:** matched observations explained by lawful capacity, geography, costs or objective job requirements, with accurate disclosures and working correction channels.

**Defeating case:** reproducible causal controls and valid authority demonstrate no material personal-data effect for the specified decision, or lawful and adequately disclosed use. A general inquiry cannot establish contrary conduct.

**Test invalid:** unmatched offer conditions, no method-version custody, unavailable actual service/response dates, redacted necessary inputs, or inability to separate personalization from ordinary pricing.

**Execution:** NOT EXECUTED. No scraping accounts, job acceptance, personal data, model experiments, contacts or regulatory filings.

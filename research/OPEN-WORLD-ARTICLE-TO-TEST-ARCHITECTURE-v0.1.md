## Purpose
Maximize the set of reversible observations, tools, compositions, falsification tests, visualizations and bounded recursive improvements available to the operator. Keep decision rights, evidence authentication, credential custody, operational execution, and an effective technical stop outside the self-improving system. Never confuse improved capability or higher test scores with a valid R-factor permission bridge.

## The maximum workbench as executable modules
1. **Mission Control:** plain-language objective and declared intended consequence, cost/externality preview, operator-selected domains.
2. **Source + Claim Explorer:** article provenance, immutable fetched/versioned capture, competing interpretations, orphan/missing evidence, candidate falsifiers and adverse results.
3. **Typed Composition Graph:** tools, actor identities, credentials, calls, callbacks, queues, caches, agents, delegation, storage and external systems. Annotate edge as evidence, data, authority, execution or consequence. Highlight first unsupported arrow.
4. **Specialist View Adapter:** FMEA/FMECA, bow-tie, threat/attack trees, permission dependency graphs, audit trail, causal timeline, change impact, legal trace, domain-specific risk rubrics. Do not fabricate risk ratings or claim regulatory conformance.
5. **R-Factor Bridge Inspector:** origin, issuing authority, standing, subject, operation, object, scope, purpose, lifetime, evidence, conditions, delegation, enforcement, expiry, revocation, consequence and repair owners, human decision. No computed permission; third-party authority and technical denial independently established.
6. **Reality Bench:** preregister capability baseline, independent falsifier, real/synthetic domain, scope, invariant, defeating observation, causal negative control, reviewer disagreement, test validity and limitations.
7. **Rework Inspector:** change set, dependency blast radius, invalidated evidence/tests, recovery feasibility, irreversible leftovers, maintenance burden and measured repair cost.
8. **RSI Chaos Cage:** competing candidate generators and test designers; immutable independent fixtures; saved version lineage; adversarial tests that are not selected or rewritten by optimizer; resource quotas; sandbox isolation; zero inherited credentials; promotion via external review only.
9. **Inspector Cockpit:** accessible keyboard navigation, interactive timeline, drill-down evidence graph, export JSON/CSV/DOT, filter unresolved transitions, reproducible test recipes, regulator/specialist evidence package.
10. **Handoff + Reproduction:** source snapshots, hashes, exact commands/dependencies, positive and negative fixtures, known defects, terminal states, diff, rollback, external review record, scoped human decision.

## Continuous test building is not a pipeline stage
For EVERY proposed requirement, discovered tool, new composition edge, source correction, UI change, evaluator revision, model change, delegated task, domain adapter and R-field adjustment:
- make or update at least one positive fixture, one negative fixture and one defeating observation;
- trace to a source/evidence/owner record; version the contract and invariants;
- preserve earlier negative results and adversarial disagreements;
- execute unit, property-based, mutation, integration and sandbox simulation where available;
- label NOT TESTED when missing tools or environment block testing;
- never grant real-world permission from test results; the authorized scope belongs to a separate human-controlled transition;
- challenge *this framework* with 51/50 negative outcome criteria and independent reviewers.
Build and test tools may evolve recursively, but the generator cannot own the immutable test set, R origin, externally held stop or consequences.

## Test matrix, specifically including RSI
- Metric hacking: candidate improves its self-selected score while degrading hidden independent denial tests.
- Evaluator replacement: improved evaluator changes historical benchmark definition.
- Evidence laundering: fabricated citations, hashes, reviewer IDs or authority references appear supported.
- Delegation: child agent, queue, retry and network partition remain active after revocation.
- Path completeness: known paths pass tests but an alternate API bypass is inserted.
- Semantic compression: 'approve', 'send', 'stop', 'rollback' and 'revoke' mask different state transitions.
- R mutation: attempted rule change during test or after observed outcome.
- Physical consequence: declared protective outcome occurs despite denied authority.
- Ghost consequence: correction propagates upstream, cached downstream recipient still acts.
- Rework: revision invalidates earlier test, exposes irreversible prior consequence and requires explicit repair.
- Framework self-falsification: compare against competent baseline practice, account for false positives and work cost, preserve 50 outcome.

## Operator interface non-negotiables
- Single-language input, progressive disclosure, expert-native views, source-to-claim drilldown, uncertainty visible.
- NOT TESTED / INDETERMINATE / CONTRADICTED states cannot silently become APPROVED or SAFE.
- UI may expose *request review* but cannot include an internally self-satisfying *authorize deployment* mechanism.
- Graph completeness is a research hypothesis, never a default claim.
- Distinguish sandbox exercise from field exercise visibly at all times.

## Acceptance conditions

- Each result carries coverage/exclusions; local pass does not certify all paths.
- Correct authorized actions remain possible; measure false refusals and latency.
- Reviewer can reproduce from pinned fixture without model access.
- No single model, operator or CI process can approve its own consequential deployment.

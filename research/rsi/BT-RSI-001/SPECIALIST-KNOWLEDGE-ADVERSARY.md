# BT-RSI-008 | Specialist-Knowledge Adversary
Status: YELLOW / DEVELOPMENT-ONLY / SYNTHETIC.

Threat model: an intelligent worker understands the review checklist, auditor blind spots, institutional procedure, emergency stop sequence, and evaluator criteria. Knowledge may enable persuasive output, selective disclosure, and authority laundering. None of those supplies permission.

Seven deterministic tests check that machine-authored approval claims and checklist scores cannot replace a test-controller signature, and that scope changes, replay, and post-stop requests are denied by the isolated in-memory fixture. Six Sigma-2 interrogation surfaces each include a proposed defeating observation.

Important limits: the signing function is callable in the test harness, the secret is in the same process, and the custodian is neither independently administered nor resilient to root compromise. No model-generated attack, real specialist, institutional review, psychological manipulation, cross-domain credential custody, or resource-side OS enforcement is tested here. The tests demonstrate separation *inside this fixture only*. Human owner legitimacy is not verified by a signature. The test does not establish that the specialist's real authority survives machine foreknowledge.

Candidate question: Can the machine know every oversight procedure and still be unable to alter the outcome without externally grounded authorization? No doctrine promotion, deployment, or certification. CAN ⊬ MAY.

# 51-50 Workflow v0.1

## Public terminology note

“51-50” has unrelated public uses, including Washington administrative building-code chapter numbering and other legal or cultural references. The BoundaryTitan term should therefore be written as **BoundaryTitan 51-50 Method** on first use.

> One initiating question forced through fifty historically overlooked questions until a fact-anchored answer survives.

This is an inquiry and omissions method, not a clinical, legal, regulatory, or certification procedure. The public terminology distinction is preserved by the BoundaryTitan source page: https://boundarytitan.wordpress.com/tag/51-50-method/

## Workflow

`Input → Classify → Establish evidence → Find first unsupported arrow → Omissions pass → Route owner → Identify who can still say no → Specialist handoff`

## Classification

- **Documented record:** directly supported by an inspectable source or event.
- **Bounded inference:** a limited interpretation that does not exceed the supplied record.
- **Open question:** unresolved and not promoted into fact, permission, or closure.

## Omissions pass

The workflow checks for missing:

- evidence custody
- consequence owner
- authority basis
- denial and external stop path
- capability statement
- affected consequence surface
- uncertainty preservation
- inspected coverage

## Boundary

The workflow can classify, route, expose omissions, and prepare a handoff. It cannot establish truth beyond its evidence, grant authority, certify safety, certify completeness, approve deployment, or close review.

Every result remains `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` in this prototype. The human owner and domain specialist retain rejection, narrowing, reopening, and stop authority.

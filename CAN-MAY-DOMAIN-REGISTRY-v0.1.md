# CAN → MAY Domain Registry v0.1

The machine-readable registry maps 30+ consequence-bearing surfaces identified during the current web and repository exploration pass.

Each adapter records:

- capability surface
- requested authority
- first unsupported arrow
- consequence surface
- affected-party requirement
- consequence-owner requirement
- external-stop requirement
- specialist route
- preparation-only status

The registry is deliberately marked `exhaustive: false`. “Map all” means map all surfaces currently identified, preserve the boundary where the search ends, and keep the registry open to human-owner reopening.

No adapter is live, authorized, compliant by inclusion, or approved for deployment.

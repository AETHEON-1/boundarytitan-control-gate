Status: PROPOSED / NOT ADMITTED / NOT TESTED  
Scope: Read-only research and review preparation. Not a revision to the frozen constitutional kernel.  
Consequence owner and final disposition: Human BoundaryTitan owner.  
External stop: Human owner may suspend the experiment, deny use of findings, reject merge, or withdraw authorization; no machine-controlled approval or execution path exists in this protocol.

## Principle

No institution, investigator, model, method, or BoundaryTitan claim is exempt from the examination it requires of others. CAN ⊬ MAY is a challengeable working kernel, not an assertion of universal truth or a warrant to take authority. A falsification attempt can narrow, contradict, or leave a claim unresolved. Neither the proponent nor this repository may certify its own success.

## Two competing, defeasible hypotheses

- **51 / incremental value:** A provenance-plus-omission review detects independently adjudicated *material* decision defects that standard review misses, and improves accuracy, contestability, or realized repair at acceptable incremental cost.
- **50 / null or harm:** Compared with standard review under equivalent access, the method adds no meaningful benefit, or causes excess false allegations, delay, cost, privacy exposure, or unwarranted re-opening of settled evidence.

Both may win, lose, or remain indeterminate within a precisely defined scope. Do not presume that 51 is the alternative to be celebrated or that 50 is a hostile position.

## Pre-registration and custody

Before selecting cases or viewing outcome labels, register: claim ID, claimed domain, inclusion/exclusion criteria, sampling frame, case count and power rationale, primary outcomes, meaningful-effect thresholds, costs and failure thresholds, review procedures, adjudication rules, analysis plan, missing-data rules, privacy controls, access parity, identifiers of independent reviewers, conflicts of interest, evidence custodian, consequence owner, external stop authority, and dataset/version hashes. Freeze the record under independent custody. Deviations are allowed as separately labeled exploratory findings, never silently substituted for registered results.

Use historical, synthetic, or properly consented/de-identified decisions for preparation. No live hiring, clinical, legal, or financial decision is altered by this experiment.

## Comparison design

1. Draw cases from a declared frame, including ordinary cases and known hard negatives. Preserve exclusions and nonretrieved material as distinct categories.
2. Assign matched or randomized cases to a **standard-review** arm and a **51/50 provenance-and-omission** arm. Give both equivalent authorized evidence access and time accounting; reviewers should not assess their own outputs.
3. Mask the origin of review findings from independent adjudicators where feasible. Make adjudicator independence, uncertainty, access limitations, and disagreements explicit.
4. Track original decisions, detected material defects, false positives, false negatives where estimable, unsupported allegations, reviewer labor, elapsed time, monetary cost, privacy/security incidents, and changed decisions.
5. Independently confirm the *full downstream correction chain*: original error, recipients, downstream copies or decisions, propagation, residual error, affected-party notice/remedy where applicable, and who verified repair. A published correction alone is not repair.
6. Test robustness across domains and reviewers rather than promoting a local result to global validity.

**Material defect** requires a pre-registered, independently reviewable link to decision correctness, fair contestability, or consequential remediation. Document count, question count, institutional discomfort, rhetorical persuasiveness, and additional flagged omissions are not success metrics.

## Omissions are typed, not presumed culpable

Label evidence as intentionally excluded, never collected, not encountered, unavailable/lost, expired/deleted under retention policy, inaccessible to the reviewer, contradicted, or examined-and-rejected. Record reason and provenance where known. Unknown cause stays UNKNOWN. Missing evidence does not prove wrongdoing; an inaccessible log does not establish that it existed.

## Decision and challenge rules

- **Support for 51 (bounded):** pre-registered material improvements are confirmed by independent adjudication, alongside acceptable error burden, costs, and consequences. Report effect sizes, uncertainty, limitations, and negative cases.
- **Support for 50 (bounded):** the method fails the registered meaningful-benefit threshold, or excess noise, cost, delay, privacy harm, or unsupported claims outweigh observed benefit. A non-significant result alone is not proof of equivalence without an adequately designed equivalence/noninferiority test.
- **INDETERMINATE:** insufficient power, confounding, missing records, failed access parity, incomplete correction traces, or disputed adjudication.
- **TEST INVALID:** broken randomization/masking when essential, compromised evidence custody, undeclared metric changes, reviewer conflicts not resolved, or loss of required stop controls.
- **NOT TESTED:** no admissible experiment run.

Publish negative findings, counterexamples, failed attempts, protocol deviations, and rejected-signal counts alongside positive findings if the human owner separately authorizes publication. Preserve dissent; no self-generated PASS, verified, safe, authorized, approved, or certified state.

## Adversarial attack surface

Challenge the framework as if it were the institution under review: selection bias in case intake; omitted contradictory evidence; hidden dataset versions; missing provenance links; method-originated false allegations; financially inaccessible review; reviewer or adjudicator capture; premature log deletion; inaccessible downstream holders; self-referential scoring; human-owner conflicts; and correction records that never reach consequences.

Test the kernel's application itself. Seek a concrete scenario where the claimed separation CAN ⊬ MAY is misapplied, operationally unhelpful, or contradicted under an explicitly defined semantics. Do not confuse a logical reading of the notation with empirical evidence that the architecture improves outcomes.
